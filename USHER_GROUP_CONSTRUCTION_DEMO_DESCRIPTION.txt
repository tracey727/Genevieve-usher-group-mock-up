GENEVIEVE App™ — Usher Group Construction Site Flow & Safety Support Assistant
DEMO ONLY — fake data only

Purpose:
This demo has been built to show how GENEVIEVE App™ could support Usher Group-style construction, painting, waterproofing, supervisors, subcontractors and site managers.

It is not designed to replace workplace health and safety systems, site supervisors, qualified trades, engineers, inspectors, certifiers, legal advisers or insurance advisers.

It is designed to sit beside the work and make risks, missed checks, fatigue, defects, materials, handovers and sign-offs visible before they become bigger problems.

What this first construction demo shows:
- daily site check-in
- worker break and fatigue check
- site hazard board
- weather and surface condition checks
- painting module
- waterproofing module
- materials and stock alerts
- defect photo log
- rework risk alerts
- supervisor sign-off
- subcontractor handover
- manager action board
- evidence export
- legal/safety wording boundaries

The Genevieve colour system:
GREEN — ready, checked, covered, stable
AMBER — check soon, caution, support or review needed
RED — do not proceed, action required, supervisor review needed
BLACK / URGENT — immediate safety pathway or stop-work escalation

This demo uses fake data only.
Do not enter real site incidents, real worker information, confidential builder data or live client information until the system has had proper legal, WHS, privacy, insurance and business review.
