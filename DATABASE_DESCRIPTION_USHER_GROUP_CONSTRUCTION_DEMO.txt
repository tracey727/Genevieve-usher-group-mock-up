DATABASE DESCRIPTION — USHER GROUP CONSTRUCTION DEMO

The construction database is designed as a site-flow, safety-support and evidence-support layer.

It does not replace legal WHS duties, qualified supervisors, site inspections, engineering sign-offs, certifiers, contracts or insurance requirements.

Suggested database sections:

1. Sites
Site ID, site name, supervisor, project type, weather exposure, active trades, site status.

2. Workers
Worker ID, role, company, fatigue check, break status, task assignment, supervisor contact.

3. Daily Site Checks
Date, site open/closed, weather, access, hazards, PPE, exclusion zones, emergency access, supervisor sign-off.

4. Hazards
Hazard type, location, photo, severity, owner, action required, due time, closed time.

5. Trade Modules
Painting, waterproofing, plastering, flooring, electrical, plumbing, carpentry, roofing, concreting, demolition, tiling, cleaning and handover.

6. Waterproofing Checks
Substrate, falls, moisture, primer, membrane coat 1, membrane coat 2, curing, penetrations, flood test, photo proof, sign-off.

7. Painting Checks
Surface prep, sanding, cleaning, primer, coat count, drying time, weather, defects, colour/product used, final sign-off.

8. Materials
Item, quantity, minimum level, supplier, site location, expiry/shelf life, reorder alert.

9. Defects / Rework
Defect, trade, photo, severity, cause, cost impact, action, close-out evidence.

10. Handovers
From trade, to trade, checklist, conditions, photos, supervisor sign-off.

11. Alerts
Green/amber/red/urgent, reason, owner, action required, due time, closed time.

12. Evidence Log
Photos, checklists, timestamps, signatures, exports and audit trail.

Real use would need WHS, legal, privacy, insurance, contract and industry review.
